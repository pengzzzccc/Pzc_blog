## **1. Evolving the Core Loop: From Sequential Downtime to Simultaneous Action**

**Wolves of Wand Street** is a 3–4 player card-driven area-control tabletop game where rival magical investment firms compete for territorial dominance using **Gold**, **Investors**, and **Action Cards**.

Across three major rule iterations, human playtesting revealed that traditional sequential turns created excessive downtime and runaway leader snowballs in a 4-player setting. I redesigned the core turn structure into a **3-round, 10-minute simultaneous-action format** with independent draw piles, keeping every player actively reading opponents and committing resources on every beat.

## **2. Automated Rule Validation with Monte Carlo Tree Search**

While in-person playtesting was invaluable for evaluating player interaction and table tension, human sessions alone could not exhaustively test edge-case card combinations across 26 distinct card types.

To rigorously verify numerical balance and rule consistency, I implemented an **automated simulation harness powered by Monte Carlo Tree Search**:

- **Deterministic State and Action Encoding**: Modelled the three-round progression, regional control thresholds, Gold and Investor resource pools, and card effects as a fast deterministic state machine.
- **Detecting Dominant Strategies**: By running thousands of simulated self-play matches, we tracked win-rate correlation per card played, simultaneous resolution fairness, and resource starvation rates.
- **Data-Driven Rebalancing**: Simulation metrics exposed that early-round economy and expansion plays created an insurmountable Investor advantage by the second round. Using these findings, we recalibrated card costs and regional scoring weights so that final-round counter-play and redeployment cards remained viable without invalidating early investments.
