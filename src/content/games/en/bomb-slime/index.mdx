## Gameplay

You play a slime that swallows bombs and spits them back out to set off chain explosions. A rope gun and wall dashes let you move through levels at speed, and levels rewind when you die.

## My role

I am the lead designer, and I also handle lead programming and level design.

- **Core gameplay design and systems**: led the design and implementation of the core gameplay, including the player's basic movement and feedback from interacting with the environment.
- **Technical architecture and tools**: built the project's main technical framework in Unity on my own, including modular scene interaction components, an event-driven player control system and a visual level testing tool.

## Tech

Unity 6 · URP (2D Renderer) · Input System · UI Toolkit
