## Core Gameplay

In *Bomb Slime*, you play a slime that reels bombs in with a **rope gun** to swallow them, then spits them back out as armed projectiles to trigger **chain explosions**. Levels combine breakable walls, zero-gravity floating bombs, timed hazards, and state-restoring checkpoints. Players chain rope swings, wall-smashing dashes, wall slides, and ceiling crawls to traverse rooms at high speed. When you die, the level itself rewinds alongside the player so shattered walls and spent bombs are restored for a clean retry.

## My Role (Team of 5)

As **Lead Designer, Lead Programmer, and Level Designer**, I owned the core mechanics, runtime system architecture, and playtest telemetry toolchain:

- **Decoupled Event-Bus Architecture (8 Static Buses + Directors)**: Architected eight domain event buses (`PlayerBus`, `LifeBus`, `ItemBus`, `HazardBus`, `FxBus`, `RopeGunBus`, `LevelBus`, `UiBus`) to decouple gameplay simulation from presentation directors (`FxDirector` for screen shake/hit-stop/zoom, `AudioDirector` with priority voice pooling, `HapticsDirector` for gamepad rumble and DualSense adaptive triggers, and `DeathDirector`/`RespawnDirector`).
- **Composable Interactable Framework & State Rewind**: Built a modular `Interactable` node system composed of orthogonal parts (`ExplodePart`, `BreakablePart`, `CarriablePart`, `FloatablePart`, `RestorablePart`, `HarmOnTouch`), paired with a `LevelMemento` system that snapshots and rewinds all `IRestorable` entities upon checkpoint respawn, and an `UnstuckSolver` to recover players wedged in geometry.
- **Playtest Telemetry & Visual Level Testing Suite (`PerfLog Viewer` & Scene Overlay)**: Engineered an in-game `PerformanceRecorder` (logging Git-stamped CSVs of movement trajectories, death coordinates/causes, mechanic usage, frame times, and per-checkpoint segment stats) alongside a standalone web analytics dashboard (**PerfLog Viewer**) and an in-editor **PerfLog Scene Overlay** that renders death heatmaps, lethality contours, ghost replays, and difficulty curves directly over exported level art.
