## **Research Goal**

During gameplay prototyping and balance tuning, backend telemetry alone rarely explains why players gravitate toward certain loops. This **Player Motivation Assessment Scale Study** pairs standardized player-profile variables with a 12-dimension subjective motivation framework, making it easier to cross-reference self-reported player drivers against playtest behavior and target-audience assumptions.

## **Scale Architecture (42 Items)**

The instrument is organized into two structured sections:

- **Part 1 · Player Profile & Calibration (Q01–Q06)**: Captures preferred input devices (keyboard and mouse or controller), primary gaming platforms (PC, console, handheld, mobile), average bi-weekly playtime over the past two months, and demographic brackets, followed by an intuitive-response confirmation check before the scale items begin.
- **Part 2 · 6-Category, 12-Dimension Core Scale (Q07–Q42)**: Uses a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly disagree, 5 = Strongly agree) with three balanced items per dimension, averaged into final 1.00–5.00 dimension scores:
  - **Escape**: Excitement & Thrills vs. Thinking & Puzzles.
  - **Exploration**: Collecting vs. Discovery.
  - **Expression**: Customization vs. Roleplay & Story.
  - **Management**: Resource Allocation vs. Strategic Planning.
  - **Mastery**: Reaching Milestones vs. Skill Improvement.
  - **Social**: Competition vs. Cooperation.

## **Interactive Survey & Export Pipeline**

The live questionnaire runs directly inside the site's new **Surveys** section behind a participant access key gate:

- **Instant 12-Axis Radar Visualization**: Upon submission, the browser computes arithmetic means across all 12 dimensions, highlights the respondent's top three drivers, and renders a theme-aware 12-axis SVG radar chart alongside a 6-category summary.
- **One-Click CSV & PNG Export**: Respondents and researchers can export both raw item-by-item responses plus computed dimension scores as UTF-8 CSV files and download high-DPI PNG snapshots of the radar chart for offline analysis and playtest reports.
