## About

A card-driven area-control tabletop game for 3–4 players. A game takes about 10 minutes over 3 rounds.

## My role

I was responsible for rules design and playtesting.

- **Rules design**: working from a given turn structure, designed the different card rules and the players' resource management system.
- **Playtesting**: built automated tests based on the game rules and the MCTS algorithm to check whether the rules hold up, and adjusted the rules based on the results.

## How to play

- Online: import `wolves_of_wand_street.pcio` from the repository into playingcards.io
- In person: print the print sheet in the repository (`Assets/Scripts/output.pdf`)
